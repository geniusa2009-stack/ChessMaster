# ChessMaster Foundations (CH01)

The beginner entry chapter — 12 lessons + a server-verified rules assessment. Generated from foundations.ts.

## Lessons
- **CH01-L001** Understanding the Board & Coordinates — Identify squares by file/rank and read algebraic coordinates. (concepts: 8×8 board, files a–h, ranks 1–8, light/dark squares, algebraic notation)
- **CH01-L002** King & Queen Movement — Move the king (one square) and the queen (lines & diagonals). (concepts: king one square, queen any distance straight/diagonal)
- **CH01-L003** Rook, Bishop & Knight Movement — Move the rook (ranks/files), bishop (diagonals) and the jumping knight. (concepts: rook straight, bishop diagonal, knight L-shape jump)
- **CH01-L004** Pawn Movement & Captures — Move pawns forward but capture diagonally. (concepts: forward move, first-move double step, diagonal capture)
- **CH01-L005** Blocking & Legal Captures — Understand that most pieces cannot pass through others. (concepts: blocked paths, friendly pieces block, enemy pieces are targets, knight exception)
- **CH01-L006** Understanding Check — Recognise when a king is in check. (concepts: king attacked, check vs ordinary attack)
- **CH01-L007** Responding to Check — Respond to check by moving, capturing or blocking. (concepts: move the king, capture the checker, block the check)
- **CH01-L008** Checkmate & Stalemate — Distinguish checkmate from stalemate. (concepts: checkmate = check + no reply, stalemate = no check + no move)
- **CH01-L009** Castling — Castle legally and recognise when castling is forbidden. (concepts: king + rook unmoved, squares empty, not through/into check)
- **CH01-L010** En Passant — Perform en passant and know its timing. (concepts: immediate opportunity, diagonal pawn capture)
- **CH01-L011** Promotion — Promote a pawn and choose the piece. (concepts: reach last rank, choose Q/R/B/N)
- **CH01-L012** Beginner Rules Assessment — Demonstrate the fundamentals in a graded, server-verified assessment. (concepts: all CH01 rules)
