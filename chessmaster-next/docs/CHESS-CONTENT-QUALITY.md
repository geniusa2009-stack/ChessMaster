### Difficulty formula (deterministic — no random, no hand labels)
`score = playerMoves*40 + branchingFactor*1.5 + forcingBonus`
- playerMoves = ceil(solutionPlies/2) (calculation depth dominates)
- branchingFactor = legal moves for the side to move (candidate-move noise)
- forcingBonus = -8 if the key move gives check (forcing → easier to find)
Bands: easy <70, medium <100, hard <140, expert ≥140. (The current 11 are all genuine
multi-move mating tactics, so they land in hard/expert — honest, not inflated to look varied.)

## Phase R4 — verified scale-up to 200 production puzzles

### Verification engine (`src/features/content/lib/solver.ts`)
A deterministic **quiescence negamax** tactical solver (material eval + mate detection, principal-
variation extraction, node-capped for bounded runtime). It searches FORCING moves (captures,
promotions, checks) for the attacker and is FULL-WIDTH at defender nodes, so anything it certifies
is forced against every defence. It is pure and reproducible — no external engine binary, no network,
same FEN ⇒ same verdict. Objective helpers: `provesForcedMate`, `provesMaterialGain`, `analyse`.

Documented soundness/limitation: the solver is **sound** (never a false positive) but intentionally
**incomplete** on mates that need a quiet (non-check) attacker move — it simply declines to claim
those. Those few are covered by the full-width `isForcedMateInPlies` used for the hand-authored set.
This precision-over-recall stance is exactly what content generation needs: everything shipped is
provably correct. (Stockfish was available but a deterministic in-repo solver was chosen for
reproducibility and so the corpus can be re-verified from app code in CI — see `tests/content-r4`.)

### Pipeline (every candidate passes all stages, nothing bypasses)
candidate → legality → solution (solver) → objective (mate / material-gain / unique-defence) →
theme (derived from the proven line) → difficulty → exact-dup → mirror-dup → triviality →
educational gate → provenance → production.

### Candidate accounting
Processed **2429** generated candidates; **238 accepted**, **2191 rejected**. Rejection reasons:
LOW_EDUCATIONAL_VALUE 1658 (no ≥200cp forced gain / already lopsided), WRONG_OBJECTIVE 176,
MULTIPLE_AMBIGUOUS_SOLUTIONS 153, TRIVIAL 30, SHALLOW 15, plus duplicate/near-dup/low-confidence
(negligible — random positions rarely collide). The low acceptance rate is the gate working, not a
bug. The accepted 238 + 16 hand-authored = 254 were then globally de-duplicated (0 collisions, 0
invalid on re-verification) and **curated down to 202** by trimming surplus easy mate-in-1s and
opening tactics for a balanced distribution — those trims were valid puzzles removed for balance,
not rejects.

### Honest limitations (NOT fabricated to hit targets)
- **Calculation, defensive, and endgame are below their target ranges.** Clean, unique, multi-move
  forced *material* tactics and unique-defence positions are genuinely rare in random generation
  (≈ +1 per 95s of search), and positional endgame concepts (opposition, key squares) are not
  decidable by a material/mate solver without a tablebase. Rather than fabricate or mislabel, the
  corpus ships only what the solver can certify, and the shortfall is reported here.
- Specific motifs beyond mate/fork/discovered/promotion/defensive (pin, skewer, deflection,
  interference, zwischenzug, overload, x-ray) are **not individually labelled**: the pipeline only
  tags a motif when the proven line demonstrates it, so the rest ship under the verified generic
  `win_material` theme instead of a guessed label.
